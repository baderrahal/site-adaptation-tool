- **R400 Record sizes.** The files a session reads at start stay small, and no record grows past what a session reads by grep. CLAUDE.md is at most 10 KB. steps/PROGRESS.md is at most 60 lines and 6 KB, with no line over 200 characters. The top entry of steps/log.md, from its ## heading to the next, is at most 4 KB.
