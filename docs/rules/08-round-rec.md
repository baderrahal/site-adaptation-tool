
## Round REC rules

- **R400 Record sizes.** The files a session reads at start stay small, and no record grows past what a session reads by grep. CLAUDE.md is at most 10 KB. steps/PROGRESS.md is at most 60 lines and 6 KB, with no line over 200 characters. The top entry of steps/log.md, from its ## heading to the next, is at most 4 KB. No file of docs/ or steps/ outside steps/history/ is over 100 KB. A KB is 1,024 bytes. A record that would pass its cap is cut into parts at line ends with no byte changed, as Round REC cut docs/rules.md into docs/rules/, docs/addin-design.md into docs/design/ and the round file of Round ADDIN 0a to 4e into steps/history/round-addin/. python/record_parts.py joins the parts in name order for a reader that needs the whole, and names every file over its cap. docs/rules/index.md, written by python/rules_index.py, lists each rule id with its bold title under its part. python/split_proof.py and steps/runs/rec/second_route.sh prove a cut by the SHA-256 of the original and of its parts joined. test_r400_record_sizes holds the caps in the gate.
