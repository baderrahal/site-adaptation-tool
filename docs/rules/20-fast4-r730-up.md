## Fast build FAST-4 of build-3, the plot read and the mirror, 6 Nov 2026

Bader's order of 6 Nov 16:25. Written by Lead from the line build-3 to Lead of steps/seam.md and PR 240, as docs/rules/ is on the list of FAST-2. A new part, so no two lanes write one rule file.

- **R730 Plot table holds villa plots.** Input: the plots of a data check, their villa type, the villa codes any source gave, the villa blocks on them. Output: plots.json plots holds each plot a villa goes on, a villa type, a villa code, also two that disagree, or a villa block. Every other numbered plot is in plots.json otherPlots with number, areaM2 and why, source computed. A read with no villa sign on any plot keeps every plot. The plotOutlines line counts the plots listed apart. read_plotTable.json follows plots. Test: dotnet/SiteAdaptation.Tests/FAST4/R730_PlotReadAndMirrorTests.cs.
- **R731 Mirror from block and footprint.** Input: the inserts of the villa block layers with mirrored, the scale sign of X times Y, their outline and the world directions of their axes, written by CadScan.Inventory with withHand. Output: a block goes to every plot its outline covers by at least half of what it covers of its best plot, CHOSEN by Code with no setting, ranked from 0 along the block axis the plots spread along, along the line through the two farthest when the insert has no axes. The mirror of each is the scale sign turned at each odd rank, weak with the reason one of n houses of one block. An insert with no outline keeps the plot that holds its point. R633 counts the hand once per insert. Test: dotnet/SiteAdaptation.Tests/FAST4/R730_PlotReadAndMirrorTests.cs.
- **R732 Pair trace.** Input: the typed plots with no villa block. Output: two plots of one type that share an edge, two corners, and share one with no other plot of their type, are a pair of opposite hands. The mirror stays missing, its reason names the other half, as the outlines do not say which half is mirrored. Test: dotnet/SiteAdaptation.Tests/FAST4/R730_PlotReadAndMirrorTests.cs.
